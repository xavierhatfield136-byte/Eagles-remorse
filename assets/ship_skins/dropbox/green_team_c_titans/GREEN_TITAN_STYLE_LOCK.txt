The approved canonical Team C titan reference set is the hull-only PNG pack in `approved/ship_hull_cutouts.zip`. Ignore the loose JPG and PNG files sitting beside it in `approved`; those are test images with bad backgrounds and should not define the style.

Use the visual language of the existing Team C green fleet already established in the project, then scale it up into Titans and a Mothership using the proportions visible in the approved cutouts. These super-capitals are not blue allied ships recolored green, and they are not generic white cathedral ships. They read as pale celadon, sea-glass, mint-silver, ivory-green, and silver-green shield warships with deep emerald and teal glazing buried inside the hull. The overall impression should be brighter and greener than the blue faction, with broad luminous midtones, calm pale armor, and only controlled dark recesses.

The approved set shows two related Team C capital-shape dialects that must still feel like one family. First: the low slab-lance shield-cruiser capitals used by Transport, Carrier Support, Bulwark, and the Mothership. These are extremely long and low, often roughly three to four times as long as they are tall, with layered deck terraces, stacked stern citadels, broad shoulder sponsons, and long elegant spear or capsule noses wrapped around luminous centerline glazing. Second: the sanctum-wing capitals used by Vanguard, Command and Intel, Boarding and Recovery, Interdiction, Shield Bastion, Fleet Teleporter, Elite Supership Command, and Hyperweapon. These are taller, usually closer to two to one, with diamond or sail-like shoulder masses, protected central naves, stained-glass shield fields, and long forward sanctum bodies projecting from a broad ceremonial power core. The Mobile Station is its own harbor variant with huge service bays, a long exposed spinal channel, and a crescent bow cradle. The Artillery Titan sits between the families as a long weapon-body attached to a fat rear shoulder block.

Keep every ship as one clean contiguous super-capital silhouette with strong bilateral symmetry. Favor long spearhead or rounded capsule noses, broad shoulder masses, narrow or protected central spines, integrated shield-lattice structures, and glazing fields set into the hull instead of painted on top. Surface treatment should feel like pale celadon ceramic-metal, mint-tinted alloy, polished sea-green armor, brushed silver-green plating, and deep emerald or teal shield windows, channel cuts, nave glazing, and luminous lattice ribs. Let the green live in translucent panes, long window bands, and shield-sail insets rather than cobalt blocks or blue navy striping.

Avoid blue-allied paint language, slate-blue armor blocks, weathered gray steel dominance, rugged industrial bunker massing, detached floating rings, exposed gun barrels, visible missiles, aircraft wings, mech legs, little escort craft, or clutter. The green Mothership should be the brightest, calmest, widest, and most authoritative expression of this same shield-warship family.
