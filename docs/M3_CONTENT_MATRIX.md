# M3 Content Matrix

## Enemy Archetypes With Counters
- `Skirmisher Patrol` (`PATROL`): focus with guns before flank.
- `Long-Range Picket` (`PICKET`): close range and break sight.
- `Stealth Raider` (`STEALTH_SHIP`): force reveal with sustained fire.
- `Interceptor` (`FIGHTER`): let CIWS + wide gun arcs thin them.
- `Strike Bomber` (`BOMBER`): prioritize before missile salvos.
- `Point-Defense Craft` (`PD_CRAFT`): use direct gun DPS, not missiles.
- `Swarm Drone` (`DRONE`): fast-gun cleanup, avoid overkill missiles.
- `Line Frigate` (`FRIGATE`): orbit and concentrate fire.
- `Siege Missile Boat` (`MISSILE_BOAT`): rush under CIWS cover.
- `CIWS Corvette` (`CIWS_CORVETTE`): beam/gun pressure over missiles.
- `Light Cruiser` (`LIGHT_CRUISER`): kite at edge of range.
- `Assault Cruiser` (`MEDIUM_CRUISER`/`CRUISER`): clear escorts, then dump missiles.
- `Battlecruiser` (`BATTLECRUISER`): avoid bow, dodge broadside arcs.
- `Battleship` (`BATTLESHIP`): flank and maintain sustained pressure.
- `Dreadnought` (`DREADNOUGHT`): phase fight, clear adds first.
- `Carrier` (`CARRIER`/`DRONE_CARRIER`): kill hull to collapse wing pressure.
- `Support Transport` (`TRANSPORT`): snipe early to remove repair aura.
- `Logistics Miner` (`MINER`): deny enemy economy.
- `Supply Hauler` (`HAULER`): intercept supply routes.
- `Defense Node` (`STATIC_TURRET`): strafe perpendicular and outrange.
- `Stronghold` (`BASE`): gain map control, then siege as group.

## Bosses With Phase Mechanics + Unique Drops
- Sector 4 `MID-BOSS ALPHA`: phased escalation + `Aegis Array` drop (`+hull`, `+shield`, `+shield regen`).
- Sector 8 `MID-BOSS BETA`: phased escalation + `Missile Core` drop (`+missile damage`, `+tracking`, `+fire rate`).
- Sector 12 `ENEMY FLAGSHIP`: phased escalation + `Flag Core` drop (`+core durability`, `+CIWS package`).
