# Campaign Titan Build Sheet

Date: 2026-03-31
Status: Draft balance sheet

## Purpose

This document turns the Titan roster into a build-ready draft with:
- Titan purchase costs
- command bonuses
- preferred deployment patterns
- an example endgame fleet mix

This is a companion to `CAMPAIGN_ESCORT_DIRECTION.md`.

## Core Rules

- The allied Mothership can field up to `8` Titans at once.
- Each Titan normally commands up to `10` ships in its deployment.
- Small Craft are attached to those deployments through carriers, hangars, or Titan support systems rather than counting as full deployment ships.
- The `Elite Supership Command Titan` is the main exception: it commands `4-5` elite Superships instead of a normal 10-ship deployment.

## Economy Assumption

These costs assume a larger campaign economy than the current early legacy campaign, with enough total credits for the player to slowly assemble a return fleet over many sectors.

If the real campaign economy stays much smaller than this draft:
- reduce all Titan costs to `50-60%` of the values below
- keep the relative ordering the same

## Per-Archetype Sheet

### 1. Transport Titan

Cost:
- `1400` credits

Command bonus:
- `+25%` salvage and cargo recovery from sector objectives in its lane
- `+20%` between-sector resupply efficiency for ships in its deployment
- `+1` emergency extraction use for crippled allied ships in that deployment

Preferred deployment:
- `2` escort cruisers
- `2` point-defense frigates
- `2` line destroyers
- `2` repair or utility frigates
- `1` sensor ship
- `1` interdiction escort

Why buy it:
- Best early logistics Titan
- Makes long campaigns less punishing by preserving resources and survivors

### 2. Bulwark Titan

Cost:
- `1800` credits

Command bonus:
- `+20%` shield durability for ships close to the Mothership
- `+15%` point-defense accuracy in its deployment
- Allies on `protect` stance take reduced breakthrough damage

Preferred deployment:
- `3` heavy line cruisers
- `2` shield frigates
- `2` point-defense destroyers
- `2` missile or assault escorts
- `1` support carrier

Why buy it:
- The default "protect the flagship" Titan
- Strongest general-purpose answer to enemy breakthrough fleets

### 3. Carrier Support Titan

Cost:
- `1900` credits

Command bonus:
- `-30%` Small Craft repair and rearm time
- `+1` upgrade tier to deployed Small Craft in its lane
- `+20%` sortie uptime for bomber and interceptor wings

Preferred deployment:
- `2` light carriers
- `2` escort frigates
- `2` interceptor destroyers
- `2` repair frigates
- `1` fleet tender
- `1` screen cruiser

Why buy it:
- Turns Small Craft into a long-campaign asset instead of disposable clutter
- Best support pick if fighters and bombers are central to the fleet identity

### 4. Vanguard Titan

Cost:
- `2100` credits

Command bonus:
- `+25%` sublight movement speed for its deployment
- `+20%` turn rate and response speed on `intercept` orders
- Rapid reaction order cooldown greatly reduced

Preferred deployment:
- `4` fast cruisers
- `2` missile destroyers
- `2` interceptor frigates
- `1` recon ship
- `1` support escort

Why buy it:
- The fleet's gap-plugger
- Excellent at chasing flankers and reinforcing failing fronts

### 5. Interdiction Titan

Cost:
- `2300` credits

Command bonus:
- Enemy retreat, warp, or jump behavior is slowed or suppressed inside its control zone
- `+20%` target lock and pursuit quality against routed enemies
- Priority targets suffer stronger slow or snare effects when focused by this deployment

Preferred deployment:
- `2` fast cruisers
- `2` electronic-warfare frigates
- `2` pursuit destroyers
- `2` interception corvettes or light escorts
- `1` sensor ship
- `1` strike carrier

Why buy it:
- Enables kills that would otherwise escape
- Pairs especially well with artillery and hyperweapon fleets

### 6. Artillery Titan

Cost:
- `2500` credits

Command bonus:
- `+25%` artillery range
- `+15%` firing-solution accuracy for long-range ships in its deployment
- First coordinated bombardment in a battle charges faster

Preferred deployment:
- `3` artillery cruisers
- `2` sensor frigates
- `2` point-defense escorts
- `2` line destroyers
- `1` shield support ship

Why buy it:
- The best siege and standoff Titan
- Breaks heavy formations before they reach the Mothership

### 7. Fleet Teleporter Titan

Cost:
- `2900` credits

Command bonus:
- Can redeploy part of a friendly deployment across the formation during battle
- Emergency reinforcement arrival time is greatly reduced
- Saves isolated ships by pulling them behind the line

Preferred deployment:
- `2` guard cruisers
- `2` fast destroyers
- `2` repair frigates
- `2` sensor or command escorts
- `1` shield frigate
- `1` supply ship

Why buy it:
- High skill ceiling
- One of the strongest anti-collapse tools in large fleet battles

### 8. Elite Supership Command Titan

Cost:
- `3200` credits for the Titan hull

Special rule:
- Commands `4-5` elite Superships instead of `10` normal ships
- Each elite Supership should also carry a high separate purchase cost

Recommended attached force:
- `4` elite Superships plus `1` recon or support command escort
- or `5` elite Superships for maximum brute-force pressure

Command bonus:
- Elite ships gain a strong coordination bonus when focusing the same target
- `+15%` subsystem performance and morale-style combat efficiency for the whole elite group
- Breakthrough orders become dramatically more lethal, but losses are expensive

Why buy it:
- Highest quality strike package in the roster
- Best when the player wants fewer, deadlier ships instead of wider coverage

### 9. Mobile Station Titan

Cost:
- `3400` credits

Command bonus:
- Functions as a deployable service node during longer operations
- Improves nearby repair, resupply, and defensive staying power
- Can anchor local defense assets or battlefield support structures if that system exists

Preferred deployment:
- `2` repair frigates
- `2` ammo or supply tenders
- `2` point-defense destroyers
- `2` shield frigates
- `1` salvage ship
- `1` escort carrier

Why buy it:
- Turns a part of the formation into a durable operating base
- Best for campaigns that emphasize attrition, staging, and recovery

Design caution:
- Keep this optional until its battlefield role is distinct from the Transport Titan and Carrier Support Titan

### 10. Hyperweapon Titan

Cost:
- `3800` credits

Command bonus:
- Carries a battle-defining weapon with long charge time and huge payoff
- Gains strong targeting bonuses against marked or interdicted enemies
- Forces the fleet to protect one expensive high-value firing platform

Preferred deployment:
- `2` shield support ships
- `2` point-defense frigates
- `2` artillery cruisers
- `2` sensor ships
- `1` repair escort
- `1` interdiction escort

Why buy it:
- The decisive late-game execution Titan
- Ideal for boss fights, fortress assaults, and final-act fleet battles

## Experimental Titan Concepts

These concepts are strong candidates for future expansion, but they are not part of the first-wave costed roster yet.

The three strongest candidates now have draft cost/spec entries in `Second-Wave Costed Candidates` below.

### 1. Command / Intel Titan

Theme:
- Battlefield brain and targeting coordinator.

Purpose:
- Extends sensor coverage.
- Improves target locks, artillery solutions, and focus-fire discipline across nearby deployments.
- Helps multiple Titans fight like one organized fleet instead of separate groups.

Best use:
- Backbone support for artillery, hyperweapon, and interdiction-heavy fleets.

### 2. Shield Bastion Titan

Theme:
- Dedicated defensive shield anchor.

Purpose:
- Projects defensive shield zones over the Mothership or nearby deployments.
- Strengthens the fleet against missile swarms, artillery fire, and breakthrough pushes.
- Serves as the best pure protection Titan after the Bulwark.

Best use:
- Defensive campaigns, convoy missions, and final pushes where survival matters more than speed.

### 3. Boarding / Recovery Titan

Theme:
- Capture, liberation, and recovery specialist.

Purpose:
- Improves disabling, boarding, and recovery operations.
- Makes it easier to free yellow ships instead of destroying them.
- Converts battlefield victories into rescued allies and reusable assets.

Best use:
- Campaigns that lean hard into the liberation theme.

### 4. ECM / Jammer Titan

Theme:
- Electronic warfare and disruption platform.

Purpose:
- Breaks enemy target locks.
- Weakens missile guidance and long-range strikes.
- Reduces the effectiveness of enemy command ships and hyperweapons.

Best use:
- Counter to precision fleets, missile doctrines, and enemy long-range formations.

### 5. Minefield / Area-Denial Titan

Theme:
- Space-control and lane-shaping platform.

Purpose:
- Deploys mines, drone pickets, and automated defenses.
- Protects weak approaches and funnels enemies into prepared kill zones.
- Makes the battlefield itself part of the fleet's defense plan.

Best use:
- Hold-the-line missions, retreat screens, and defensive sector objectives.

### 6. Siegebreaker Titan

Theme:
- Close-range fortress killer.

Purpose:
- Smashes stations, fixed defenses, enemy Titans, and other hardened objectives.
- Trades speed and flexibility for crushing short-range assault power.
- Gives the fleet a dedicated breaching Titan for major set-piece battles.

Best use:
- Station assaults, Titan hunts, and the final Earth offensive.

### 7. Drone Foundry Titan

Theme:
- Drone manufacture and battlefield automation hub.

Purpose:
- Produces disposable drones for screening, repairs, decoys, and attack runs.
- Gives the fleet extra tactical bodies without needing full-size ships.
- Can offset losses in long sectors by generating temporary support assets.

Best use:
- Attrition-heavy campaigns and fleets built around endurance.

### 8. Stealth / Decoy Titan

Theme:
- Deception and signature-control platform.

Purpose:
- Creates false targets, masks friendly deployments, and supports ambushes or withdrawals.
- Lets parts of the fleet disappear, reposition, or bait enemies into traps.
- Adds asymmetric tools to a fleet that otherwise fights in open formation.

Best use:
- High-skill campaigns, ambush play, and deception-heavy mission design.

### 9. Salvage Titan

Theme:
- Battlefield reclamation and rebuild platform.

Purpose:
- Harvests wreckage and battlefield resources at a much higher rate.
- Turns big battles into opportunities to rebuild the fleet faster.
- Helps a damaged campaign recover momentum after costly wins.

Best use:
- Long campaigns with heavy attrition and limited replacement windows.

### 10. Doctrine Titan

Theme:
- Fleet-wide tactical philosophy ship.

Purpose:
- Improves formation behavior through command doctrines like aggressive push, hold line, or precision strike.
- Changes how nearby Titans and Standard Ships behave rather than simply buffing stats.
- Gives the player a way to shape the entire fleet's combat personality.

Best use:
- Campaigns where command style and formation identity matter as much as raw ship count.

Recommended experimental priorities:
1. Command / Intel Titan
2. Boarding / Recovery Titan
3. Shield Bastion Titan

Why:
- They add clear new jobs without overlapping too heavily with the first-wave roster.
- They strengthen your core campaign themes of formation command, survival, and liberation.

|---|---:|---|---:|---|
| Command / Intel Titan | 2400 | Mid | 10 | Sensor command, targeting coordination |

### 11. Command / Intel Titan

Cost:
- `2400` credits

Command bonus:
- `+30%` sensor reach and threat detection in its command lane
- `+20%` target lock quality and shared targeting data for nearby allied deployments
- `+15%` artillery, hyperweapon, and long-range firing-solution accuracy against marked targets

Preferred deployment:
- `2` sensor cruisers
- `2` command or electronic-warfare frigates
- `2` line destroyers
- `2` point-defense escorts
- `1` artillery cruiser
- `1` recon or signals ship

Why buy it:
- Makes the whole fleet feel smarter and more coordinated
- Best support pick for artillery, hyperweapon, interdiction, and large-fleet control play

Design note:
- This should feel like the fleet's brain, not just a passive stat booster

### 12. Boarding / Recovery Titan

Cost:
- `2450` credits

Command bonus:
- `+25%` disablement efficiency against enemy subsystems and engines
- `+20%` boarding, capture, and liberation success against yellow-controlled ships
- Recoverable enemy hulks and rescued allied assets return more value after battle

Preferred deployment:
- `2` interdiction cruisers
- `2` boarding or troop frigates
- `2` disablement destroyers
- `2` repair or salvage escorts
- `1` support carrier
- `1` sensor ship

Why buy it:
- Best Titan for your liberation theme
- Turns "don't kill that ship yet" into a real fleet strategy instead of a side gimmick

Design note:
- This Titan becomes much stronger if yellow-ship liberation is a major campaign pillar

### 13. Shield Bastion Titan

Cost:
- `2600` credits

Command bonus:
- Projects layered shield coverage over the Mothership or nearby allied deployments
- `+25%` survivability against missile salvos, artillery, and alpha strikes in its protection radius
- Reinforces `hold` and `protect` stances by reducing spike damage during breakthrough attempts

Preferred deployment:
- `3` shield frigates
- `2` point-defense destroyers
- `2` heavy line cruisers
- `2` repair or support escorts
- `1` command escort

Why buy it:
- The best pure fleet-preservation Titan after the Bulwark
- Excellent for convoy defense, hold-the-line missions, and final-act survival fights

Design note:
- This should feel visually distinct from the Bulwark by protecting an area, not just tanking damage itself

## Draft Supership Rule

The `Elite Supership Command Titan` only works if Superships are rare and expensive enough to feel special.

Draft recommendation:
- Elite Supership purchase cost: `900-1100` credits each
- Hard cap per elite deployment: `5`
- Very high repair cost if lost or crippled

## Example 8-Titan Endgame Fleet

Balanced return-to-Earth fleet:
- `1` Bulwark Titan
- `1` Carrier Support Titan
- `1` Artillery Titan
- `1` Interdiction Titan
- `1` Fleet Teleporter Titan
- `1` Vanguard Titan
- `1` Elite Supership Command Titan
- `1` Hyperweapon Titan

More defensive alternate:
- Replace `Hyperweapon Titan` with `Mobile Station Titan`

More logistics-heavy alternate:
- Replace `Elite Supership Command Titan` with `Transport Titan`

## Purchasing Guidance

Suggested early purchase order:
1. Bulwark Titan or Transport Titan
2. Carrier Support Titan or Vanguard Titan
3. Artillery Titan

Suggested midgame additions:
1. Interdiction Titan
2. Fleet Teleporter Titan
3. A second frontline or support Titan depending losses

Suggested late-game additions:
1. Elite Supership Command Titan
2. Hyperweapon Titan or Mobile Station Titan

## What To Prototype First

Best first three Titans to prototype:
1. Bulwark Titan
2. Carrier Support Titan
3. Vanguard Titan

Why:
- They create immediately readable battlefield jobs
- They test the command system without needing exotic mechanics first
- They cover defense, sustainment, and rapid response, which are the three most important formation behaviors early
