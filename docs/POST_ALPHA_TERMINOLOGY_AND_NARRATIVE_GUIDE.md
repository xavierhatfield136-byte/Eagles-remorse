# Post-Alpha Terminology And Narrative Guide

## Factions and coalitions

- **Bright Yellow**: the Yellow successor aligned with the Blue/Green coalition.
  Never shorten it to “Yellow” when Dark Orange-Yellow could be present. Use its
  bright-sun insignia and diagonal-light map pattern with the name.
- **Dark Orange-Yellow**: the Yellow successor aligned with Red. Use its
  split-crown insignia and crosshatched-dark map pattern with the name.
- **Blue/Green coalition**: cooperating sovereign factions, not a merged state.
- **Red bloc**: Red and dependent or allied Dark Orange-Yellow forces. Do not
  imply that their political interests are identical.
- **Legacy Yellow**: migration-only term for alpha saves and old content IDs.
  It is never a current active faction.

## Territorial states

- **Owner** is the recognized political claimant; **controller** is the force
  exercising current military control.
- **Secure, Pressured, Contested, Occupied, Integrated** are ordered control
  states. Occupied is not the same as politically integrated.
- **Supplied, Strained, Undersupplied, Isolated, Collapsing** describe route and
  logistics state, not morale.
- A **beachhead** is an explicitly authorized, costly, finite exception that
  still expands only through adjacent edges. It is not map teleportation.

## Operations

- A **raid** damages an objective but never transfers ownership directly.
- An **invasion** is a supplied capture commitment from an adjacent controlled
  origin or valid supplied beachhead.
- Relief, reinforcement, consolidation, withdrawal, evacuation, convoy,
  interdiction, reconnaissance, sabotage, and counter-sabotage use the common
  operation record: actor, sponsor, origin, target, objective, fleet, supply,
  readiness, timing, progress, outcome, and consequences.

Every brief states: intent; why the target is legal or rejected; stakes; time;
known intelligence; uncertainty; possible consequences; and withdrawal rule.

## Epistemic labels

- **CONFIRMED FACT**: directly recorded authoritative simulation state.
- **INTELLIGENCE**: information available to the observing faction, with source
  and confidence.
- **ALLEGATION**: an attributed claim that has not been verified.
- **INTERPRETATION**: an explicitly attributed reading of confirmed facts.
- **UNKNOWN**: absent information; never fill it with invented certainty.

Faction news and campaign history may change emphasis, but their factual clauses
must cite structured event IDs. Generated summaries may compress facts and name
turning points; they must not invent dialogue, motives, casualties, participants,
or outcomes. The UI must expose the source event IDs behind a summary.

## Writing density

Write decision-bearing text: legal reason, risk, timing, consequence, or useful
character perspective. Remove generic chatter that repeats visible state.
Quiet mode suppresses optional reactions while retaining complete captions,
orders, warnings, and reports. Reduced narrative density shows one decisive
factor and a link to the full factual record.

## Civil-war review

Both Yellow successors share hulls, culture, families, and industrial history
while pursuing different political programs. Neither is merely a color swap.
Show internal disagreement, foreign pressure, civilian needs, obligations,
legitimacy, defections, and negotiated outcomes. Do not make collective guilt,
ethnicity, or identical equipment a substitute for evidence of allegiance.
