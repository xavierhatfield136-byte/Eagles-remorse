# Comm And Campaign Next Steps

This note captures the most useful follow-up work after the current comms and campaign readability pass.

## Immediate Campaign UX

- Add a short pre-mission intro card for each sector with:
  - `Win condition`
  - `Failure condition`
  - `Why this mission matters`
  - `Suggested first move`
- Make the objective card expandable:
  - compact in combat
  - detailed on hover or map-open
- Add clearer mission-section markers on the main HUD:
  - `Current pocket`
  - `Next pocket`
  - `Progress paused until arrival` when transit is required
- Add a first-mission tuning pass to confirm mission 1 is actually teachable and survivable.

## Comm System Follow-Ups

- Add a small comm result panel near the target HUD:
  - `intel shared`
  - `support acknowledged`
  - `trade cooldown`
  - `surrender accepted`
- Give `State Intent` stronger informational payoff:
  - reveal a nearby hostile
  - reveal a discovery pocket
  - hint at incoming reserves
- Expand surrender outcomes:
  - panic retreat
  - weapon shutdown
  - temporary ceasefire
  - full defection only for damaged light/support hulls
- Add faction memory so repeated hails affect trust, fear, and cooperation.

## Campaign Content Clarity

- Revisit all strategic objective texts and rewrite them as:
  - one short action verb line
  - one short tactical hint line
  - one short threat line
- Reduce repeated phrases like `fresh contacts entering from the far pocket`.
- Keep lore in hover text or transition overlays, not in the live combat objective panel.

## AI And Response Depth

- Let friendly ships actually respond to support hails with:
  - warp-to-assist behavior
  - focus-fire help
  - escort posture around the Mothership
- Let scouts break contact more often if warned off and heavily pressured.
- Let neutrals sometimes refuse or charge extra for trade if under fire.

## Playtest Questions

- Is mission 1 readable within 10 seconds of spawning?
- Can a player tell whether the mission is `survive`, `destroy`, or `escort` immediately?
- When progress pauses between pockets, is that understood as intended behavior?
- Do comm hails feel helpful enough to remember using in combat?
