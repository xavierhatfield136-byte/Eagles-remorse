# Yellow Civil War Starting Geography

The legacy Yellow state fractured along institutions and supply commitments, not hull type. Both successors therefore use the same Yellow ship catalog while their territory, transponders, insignia, doctrine, and alliances identify political control.

| Territory | Starting alignment | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Bright Yellow Frontier | Bright Yellow / Blue-Green coalition | Frontier councils depended on Green relief escorts and adopted civilian-protection guarantees after rejecting Red requisitions. It is a viable supply origin and the coalition-facing relief route. |
| Dark Yellow Lunar Hold | Dark Orange-Yellow / Red alliance | Lunar security command retained centralized emergency powers, Red ammunition contracts, and the heavy defensive network around the gravity well. It is defensible but politically brittle. |
| Southern Shelter | Blue-Green coalition | The shelter is outside the Yellow homeland and supplies Bright-aligned relief traffic without becoming Yellow-owned territory. |
| Sol Defense Net | Red | Red controls the upper strategic objective and pressures the Dark successor through military aid rather than direct ownership of the Yellow homeland. |
| Deep-Space Echo | Transitional / unaffiliated | The hidden route has no stable civic government. It supports intelligence, pirate, and false-transponder incidents without granting either successor a free territorial bridge. |

The Bright Frontier and Lunar Hold share one legal graph edge, producing a readable opening front. Neither successor can jump to Sol or the Southern Shelter without first gaining a legal adjacent staging territory. Foreign aid changes obligations and readiness but never silently transfers ownership.
