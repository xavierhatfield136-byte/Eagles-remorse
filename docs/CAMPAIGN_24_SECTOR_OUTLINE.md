# Campaign 24-Sector Outline

Date: 2026-04-13
Status: Draft expansion outline

## Purpose

This document expands the campaign from `12` sectors to `24` while keeping the existing lore, tone, and chain-of-command fantasy intact.

North star:
- Keep the campaign focused on the long road home.
- Keep the Blue Mothership as the fleet's strategic anchor.
- Keep the coalition-building arc with green alliance support and yellow liberation.
- Remove static capture-ring waits as a primary mission pattern.
- Keep escort pressure, but only in short breakout windows and with escort ships tucked into the Mothership's shadow.

## Expansion Principles

The `24`-sector version should feel like a more detailed campaign, not a padded one.

Design rules:
- Expand big story beats into paired operations: open the lane, then exploit the opening.
- Use inhabited places, orbital infrastructure, and civilian stakes as mission context.
- Prefer active objectives: break through, intercept, liberate, cover defections, silence batteries, recover assets.
- Keep boss missions as climactic peaks, not the default pattern.
- Keep true escort missions short: `100s` max, with the escorted Titan riding close to the Mothership.
- Replace stationary capture holds with staged destroy, relief-break, or extraction objectives.

## Mission Families

The expanded campaign should reuse a small set of mission families instead of inventing one-off gimmicks.

- `Survive / Rearguard`: hold long enough for the fleet to assemble, spool, or evacuate.
- `Breakthrough`: destroy the screen, then destroy the relief wing trying to close the lane again.
- `Intercept`: kill a hunter pack or command element before it reaches an allied asset.
- `Jammer Hunt`: destroy sensor, comms, or jamming nodes that are blinding the coalition.
- `Liberation`: destroy prison tenders, clamps, tractor nodes, or jailer escorts to free allied ships.
- `Defection Cover`: keep enough neutral or allied survivors alive while they cross into coalition protection.
- `Counterbattery`: silence artillery towers, anchor guns, or orbital control nodes.
- `Recovery Sweep`: secure archives, fuel caskets, medical hulls, or state assets before demolition ships destroy them.
- `Escort Breakout`: a short escort under heavy pressure once the asset has already fallen into formation.
- `Boss`: kill a Titan or Mothership anchoring the enemy operation.

## Structure

Recommended structure:
- Keep the existing `3` acts.
- Expand each act from `4` sectors to `8`.
- Use paired operations to turn current broad beats into full sequences.

Act split:
- `Act I`: sectors `1-8`
- `Act II`: sectors `9-16`
- `Act III`: sectors `17-24`

## Act I: Trade Hub Collapse

### Sector 1

`ANCHORAGE FIRESTORM`
- Location: `Far Trade Anchorage`
- Family: `Survive`
- Objective: Hold the evacuation lanes while the colony burns and civilian hulls clear the harbor.
- Purpose: Establish scale, panic, and the fact that the fleet is fighting to preserve survivors, not just win battles.

### Sector 2

`CUSTOMS HALO COLLAPSE`
- Location: `Outer Colony Jump Ring Approach`
- Family: `Intercept`
- Objective: Destroy customs-halo gunships and interdiction cutters before they seal the civilian aperture.
- Purpose: Shows that the AI is weaponizing civilian infrastructure.

### Sector 3

`BREAKOUT VECTOR`
- Location: `Outer Colony Jump Ring`
- Family: `Breakthrough`
- Objective: Break the red interdiction cordon at the aperture itself.
- Purpose: Preserves the current breakout beat but gives it its own full operation.

### Sector 4

`LAST AUTHORITY RELAY`
- Location: `Gate Relay Tethys`
- Family: `Counterbattery / Breakthrough`
- Objective: Destroy the route-control blockers pinning the relay.
- Purpose: Opens the legal Earthward route without asking the player to sit in a ring.

### Sector 5

`RELAY RELIEF BREAK`
- Location: `Tethys Relay Hinterlane`
- Family: `Breakthrough`
- Objective: Destroy the reserve wing racing the relay to shut the route again.
- Purpose: Turns the old "hold the uplink" phase into a moving, aggressive follow-through.

### Sector 6

`DEBRIS WAKE RECOVERY`
- Location: `Burning Debris Wake`
- Family: `Recovery Sweep`
- Objective: Recover state archives, fuel caskets, and convoy stragglers before scuttling ships erase them.
- Purpose: Adds a human and political stake between the breakout and the pursuit Titan.

### Sector 7

`RED KNIFE PURSUIT`
- Location: `Shattered Traffic Lanes`
- Family: `Boss`
- Objective: Destroy the AI pursuit Titan before it catches the refugee column.
- Purpose: Keeps the first major Titan kill as the act climax.

### Sector 8

`REFUGEE WAYLINE`
- Location: `Civilian Exodus Corridor`
- Family: `Escort Breakout`
- Objective: Keep the Exodus Transport Titan under the Mothership's screen through a short breakout window.
- Purpose: Preserves escort tension in its best form: compressed, urgent, and mobile.

`GREEN CONTRACT FRONT`
- Location: `Coalition Array Nysa`

- Objective: Destroy the jammer triad around the array.

`NYSA RELIEF BREAK`
- Location: `Contract Array Rear Orbit`

- Objective: Destroy the red relief wing and command ship trying to re-isolate the array.

### Sector 15

`KHARON OUTER SCREEN`
- Location: `Siege Gate Kharon`
- Family: `Counterbattery`

### Sector 16

`ASHEN GATE`
- Location: `Siege Gate Furnace`
- Family: `Boss`

## Act III: Return To Earth

- Location: `Luna Perimeter`

### Sector 22

- Location: `Earth Approach Lane`

### Sector 23

### Sector 24

- Purpose: Ends the campaign where it should end: not with another lane fight, but with liberation.

## Mission Construction Notes

The first implementation pass should not add twelve brand-new systems at once.

Recommended rule:
- Build a few reusable mission frameworks.
- Author the `24` sectors as combinations of those frameworks.
- Promote a mission family to a true objective type only if we repeat it enough to justify dedicated logic.

## Reusable Systems To Build

### 1. Staged Breakthrough Framework

Use for sectors `3`, `5`, `14`, and `22`.

Needs:
- Stage `0`: destroy authored blockers or anchors.
- Stage `1`: kill a relief wing or command ship.
- Shared HUD phase text and banners.
- Shared reward handling for a clean breakthrough.

Implementation note:
- This can stay inside scripted `DESTROY` flow at first.

### 2. Intercept Framework

Use for sectors `2` and `17`.

Needs:
- A hostile strike group with a visible target asset or target lane.
- A timer or progress meter showing how close the strike group is to the protected asset.
- Failure or penalty if the hunter pack reaches the target alive.

Implementation note:
- This likely deserves a dedicated objective type once more than one sector uses it.

### 3. Liberation Framework

Use for sectors `19`, `20`, and `23`.

Needs:
- Captured allied assets starting neutralized, disabled, or hostile-controlled.
- Prison tenders, clamps, tractor nodes, or jailer escorts as objective pieces.
- Faction swap or allied activation once the control pieces are destroyed.
- Automatic formation snap to the Mothership after liberation.

Implementation note:
- The new escort-follow behavior already solves part of the post-liberation follow state.

### 4. Defection Cover Framework

Use for sectors `9` and `12`.

Needs:
- Neutral or green hulls crossing from danger into coalition protection.
- Survivor thresholds such as `3 of 5 must survive`.
- Coalition favor or fleet reward scaling from survivor count.

Implementation note:
- This can be built around objective assets plus a survivor quota before it becomes a dedicated type.

### 5. Recovery Sweep Framework

Use for sectors `6` and `11`.

Needs:
- Named civilian or state assets that can be recovered or preserved.
- Demolition ships or scuttling teams that threaten those assets.
- Clear reward text tied to what was saved.

Implementation note:
- This is a good place to feed the campaign economy and doctrine route.

### 6. Counterbattery Framework

Use for sectors `4`, `13`, `15`, and `21`.

Needs:
- Static towers, anchors, jammer nodes, or artillery spotters as authored hostile assets.
- Optional escalating reinforcements while the batteries remain online.
- Distinct warnings so the player understands why the batteries matter.

Implementation note:
- Current authored `DESTROY` scripting already covers much of this.

### 7. Rearguard Extraction Framework

Use for sector `18` and any later convoy-defense beats.

Needs:
- A moving assembly, jump spool, or extraction condition.
- Pressure waves that feel like converging attackers, not ring babysitting.
- Optional mobile fallback line or jump anchor instead of a stationary capture zone.

Implementation note:
- This can reuse `SURVIVE` progress, but should add a moving narrative anchor.

## Supporting Features

These features make the new mission families feel coherent.

- `Generalized sector staging`: reusable phase and wave helpers instead of custom one-offs for every sector.
- `Mobile objective anchors`: objective text and world markers that can follow ships, corridors, or rally lines.
- `Survivor quotas`: success based on how many allied assets remain alive.
- `Liberation state swaps`: convert neutralized or hostile-controlled hulls into active allies during play.
- `Approach pressure HUD`: show when hunter packs, relief wings, or demolition teams are closing on an asset.
- `Outcome-sensitive rewards`: scale credits, favor, and doctrine score based on what was saved.
- `Expanded backdrop authoring`: each paired operation should feel like a different place or orbital layer, not the same wallpaper twice.
- `Checkpoint and reward continuity`: the campaign should remember who was saved, which coalition tier advanced, and which liberated ships joined the fleet.

## Construction Order

### Phase 1: Data And Structure

- Expand campaign sector count to `24`.
- Keep the `3`-act structure and remap sector-to-act pacing.
- Create sector, side-objective, and lore placeholders for all `24` sectors.
- Add a sector-pair planning sheet so old `12`-sector beats map cleanly into new paired operations.

### Phase 2: Reusable Mission Frameworks

- Generalize staged `DESTROY` missions for breakthrough and counterbattery.
- Build survivor/objective-asset quota helpers.
- Build liberation activation and faction-swap support.
- Build intercept target-progress support.

### Phase 3: Expand The Existing Middle Beats

Build the sectors that already have strong lore anchors first:
- `4-5` relay pair
- `13-14` Nysa pair
- `19-20` yellow liberation pair
- `21-22` Luna pair

These sectors are the cleanest early wins because they already exist in some form in the current campaign.

### Phase 4: Add The Missing Connective Operations

- Add the new recovery, defection, and intercept sectors that give the campaign breathing room between major battles.
- Add economy/favor rewards tied to saved assets and defecting survivors.
- Author the new backdrops and landmark text for those sectors.

### Phase 5: Final Earth Sequence

- Build sector `23` as the final setup beat that weakens the AI over Earth.
- Rebalance sector `24` so the final Mothership battle remains the ultimate climax.
- Tune coalition fleet presence, liberated yellow support, and doctrine-route payoff.

## First Recommended Build Slice

If we want the fastest path to a believable `24`-sector campaign, build this slice first:

1. Generalized staged destroy framework.
2. Generalized survivor/objective-asset tracking.
3. Liberation activation and follow-state behavior.
4. Sector pairs `4-5`, `13-14`, `19-20`, and `21-22`.

That slice gives us:
- Active replacements for the worst hold missions.
- A cleaner green-house alliance arc.
- A full yellow liberation arc.
- A stronger Luna-to-Earth finale.

## Working Test Rule

Every new mission family should answer these questions before content authoring is considered done:

- Is the objective readable in one HUD line?
- Does it keep the Mothership at the center of the fleet fantasy?
- Does it avoid asking the player to stand still unless the story absolutely requires it?
- Does it strengthen coalition growth, liberation, convoy protection, or the return to Earth?
- Can it be reused in at least two sectors, or should it stay a one-off scripted beat?
